## 1. How to fill in this record

**Grade scale (absolute, never curved)**

| Total score | Grade | Letter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ≥ 90% | 5 | A |
| 80–89% | 4 | B |
| 70–79% | 3 | C |
| 65–69% | 2 | D |
| 60–64% | 1 | E |
| < 60% | 0 | F (fail) |

A course also fails if the final exam score is below 50%, whatever the total.

**Component templates (the `Components` column)**

| Type | Components and weights |
| --- | --- |
| LEC — lecture course with posted exams or notes only (MIT OCW, Yale, NPTEL web courses) | PS __/20 · Mid __/30 · Fin __/50 |
| NPTEL — NPTEL video course | Asg __/25 · Fin __/75 |
| MOOC — Coursera/edX in audit mode | Mod __/40 · Fin __/60 |
| LAB — lab or experimental project | Plan __/20 · Rep __/50 · Oral __/30 |
| RUB — capstone, thesis proposal, thesis | Rubric __/5 (weighted mean of the five rubric criteria) |
| SRC — elective, breadth or track course | Use the template matching the source you choose; state it in the row |

**Column rules**

- **Date:** date the final exam (or final deliverable) was completed, YYYY-MM-DD.
- **Att.:** attempt number (1 or 2). A retake gets its own row; never overwrite the first attempt.
- **Evidence:** links to the scanned marked exam, code repository and/or report. No evidence, no grade.
- **NPTEL proctored score:** if you sat the NPTEL certification exam, enter its score as `Fin` and link the certificate.

**Weighted mean (per semester, cumulative and for the entire programme)**

Weighted mean = Σ(grade × ECTS) ÷ Σ ECTS

- Count only passed courses (grade ≥ 1), using the passing attempt. Failed attempts stay listed but are excluded from the mean and from ECTS earned.
- Report means to two decimals.
- Spreadsheet formula (Excel / Google Sheets): `=SUMPRODUCT(GradeRange, ECTSRange) / SUM(ECTSRange)` over passed rows only.
- **Worked check:** five 6-ECTS courses graded 5, 4, 3, 5, 4 → Σ(grade × ECTS) = (5+4+3+5+4) × 6 = 126; mean = 126 ÷ 30 = **4.20**.
