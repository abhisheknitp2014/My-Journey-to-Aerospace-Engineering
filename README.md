# My Journey to Aerospace Engineering

This repository documents my journey toward becoming a self-taught aerospace engineer using AI-assisted learning.

## About This Project

Artificial intelligence is expected to have a significant impact on education by enabling personalized learning paths, customized study plans, and on-demand tutoring. As someone who has long been interested in aerospace engineering but never had the opportunity to pursue a formal degree in the field, I decided to explore whether AI could help bridge that gap.

My goal is to follow a structured aerospace engineering curriculum similar to what students would study in a university bachelor's and master's degree program, while learning independently at my own pace.

## How This Curriculum Was Created

I used Claude (Opus 5.5 Effort: Medium) as a study-planning assistant and asked it to design a comprehensive aerospace engineering learning path (3+1 Year Bachelor's and 2 Year Master's version).

### Initial Prompt

> I want to become a self-taught Aerospace Engineer. I don't have time to attend university. Give me a 3-year study plan covering the courses typically taken by Aerospace Engineering bachelor's students. Organize the plan by year, semester, and study period similar to a university curriculum. Include freely available video/audio lectures and reading materials from platforms such as NPTEL, Coursera, and other educational resources.

### Follow-Up Prompt

> Add a fourth year with additional courses that you think would be critical after completing the suggested first three years.

### Final Refinement

> Also suggest a subsequent curriculum for a Master's degree. Follow the same instructions as above when planning.

## Repository Purpose

This repository serves as a learning journal where I will:

- Record completed courses and projects.
- Share resources and references.
- Reflect on challenges, lessons learned, and milestones achieved.

## Disclaimer

This curriculum was generated with the assistance of AI and is intended as a self-study roadmap. It is not a substitute for an accredited aerospace engineering degree. The purpose of this project is personal education, skill development, and exploration of aerospace engineering concepts through structured independent study.
